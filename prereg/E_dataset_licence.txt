=== 1. CALCE CS2 and CX2 (University of Maryland, CALCE battery data archive) ===
Pages: https://calce.umd.edu/data (current, footer "(c) 2026 University of Maryland")
       https://web.calce.umd.edu/batteries/data/ (older page, footer "Copyright (c) 2014 by CALCE and the University of Maryland, All Rights Reserved")
       https://web.calce.umd.edu/batteries/data.htm (only a redirect notice)
Licence / redistribution clause: NONE found on either page.
Only usage text (verbatim as quoted):
  current: "We provide open access to our experimental test data on lithium-ion batteries, which includes continuous full and partial cycling, storage, dynamic driving profiles, open circuit voltage measurements, and impedance measurements."
  current: "Use of this data for publication purposes should include references to the CALCE article(s) that describe the experiments conducted to generating the data."
  older:   "Usage of this data for publication purposes should include references to the CALCE battery data base and at least 3 articles referenced by CALCE and/or other contributors to this data collective."
The older page footer links to /general/web_disclaimer.htm (NOT read). Another page (IEEE DataPort "calce battery group 0",
https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/calce-battery-group-0) asks: "Please cite the link to use this dataset:https://web.calce.umd.edu/batteries/data.htm" (from a search snippet).
A Hugging Face mirror card states "license: other" because the source terms are custom (search snippet, https://huggingface.co/datasets/bsebench-org/calce-cx2-prismatic-aging-raw) - not CALCE's own statement.
=> R1: no explicit licence. Same situation as NASA PCoE ("License not specified"). Needs the user's decision.

=== 2. XJTU battery dataset (Wang et al., Nat Commun 15, 4332 (2024)) ===
Pages: https://zenodo.org/records/10963339 ; https://wang-fujin.github.io/
Zenodo, Rights: "License" -> "Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International" (cc-by-4.0), Access "Open".
Zenodo: "If you find this data helpful, please consider citing our paper:" Wang, F., Zhai, Z., Zhao, Z. et al. Physics-informed neural network for lithium-ion battery degradation stable modeling and prognosis. Nat Commun 15, 4332 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48779-z
Wang site footer: "Copyright (c) 2023. Xi'an Jiaotong University All rights reserved."  -> conflicts with CC BY 4.0 on Zenodo; neither page says which governs.
Zenodo: "Other details can be found in "Data Introduction.pdf" file." (inside the 2.4 GB zip, NOT read)
=> R1: CC BY 4.0 on the hosting record; caveat on the conflicting footer.
